LMP SOLITAIRE • ANDROID

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Luna Moon Projects LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains how the paid Android version of LMP Solitaire (version 1.0) handles player information.

1. Who operates the game

LMP Solitaire is developed and published by Luna Moon Projects LLC. For privacy questions, contact support@lunamoonprojectsllc.com.

2. Gameplay and information stored on your device

LMP Solitaire is designed to be played offline without creating an online account. Game preferences, progress, statistics, and the in-game player profile are handled locally on your device. The player profile is not an online LMP Hub account.

3. Information collected or shared by LMP Solitaire

In the version described by this policy, the game does not intentionally send your gameplay statistics, local profile, or settings to Luna Moon Projects LLC servers. It does not contain in-game advertising or integrated advertising or analytics services. Luna Moon Projects LLC does not sell player personal information.

4. Google Play and device services

If you purchase or download LMP Solitaire through Google Play, Google processes the store transaction and related account information under Google's own policies. Luna Moon Projects LLC does not receive your full payment-card details. Google may provide the developer with purchase, financial reporting, installation, or performance information through Play Console. Your Android operating system and device manufacturer may also process data independently of the game.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy for Google's data practices.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until it is cleared or removed. You may delete the game's local data using Android's app storage settings or by uninstalling the game. Device backups may preserve some app data according to your device and backup settings. The game does not provide an LMP-hosted player account that requires a separate account-deletion request.

6. Security

The game is designed to keep gameplay information on your device rather than upload it to LMP servers. Please keep your device and operating system updated and use device security controls to protect locally stored information.

7. External websites

If you choose to leave the game to visit an external website, including Luna Moon Projects LLC's website, that website may have different privacy practices. This game-specific policy does not describe any separate LMP Hub account or website service.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game's features or data practices change. A revised version will be posted here with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or LMP Solitaire, email support@lunamoonprojectsllc.com.
