ABOUT US

A little bit of everything, built with purpose.

Luna Moon Projects LLC grew out of a simple habit: if there was something I wanted to build, learn, improve, or create, I wanted to figure out how to make it real.

Today that means working across both digital and hands-on projects. One day might involve developing an app or game, while another might be spent designing a custom engraving, personalized gift, or project for another small business.

Luna Moon Projects is intentionally broad because creativity does not fit neatly into one category. The goal is to keep learning, keep building, and create things that are useful, enjoyable, personal, or simply worth making.

“Where creativity becomes reality.”
